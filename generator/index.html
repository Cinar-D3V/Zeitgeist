Do you want this carved across your body?


In In the Penal Colony, a machine, as shown below, uses needles to carve the law that a condemned man broke into his skin over twelve hours, using a very complicated writing style that the Traveler can’t even read. Prisoners are never told their sentence or given a trial; they are supposed to understand what they did wrong only through the machine carving the sentence into their body. This page puts you in the place of the condemned man, where you get to choose your crimes, and the machine gives you the same complicated and unreadable sentence for all of them. This connects to Kafka’s story because the machine punishes everyone the same way, even a soldier who simply fell asleep while on duty, showing how the machine does not care about how serious the crime actually is.
